# CELL CYCLE
**Interphase** - Takes up 90% of the Cell Cycle
- G<sub>1</sub> :  The first gap
     - ==cell grows==
     - cell monitors internal & external conditions
     - organelles are copied (NOT DNA)
- S :  Synthesis
	 - DNA replication
	 - chromosomes -> ==2 sister chromatids== connected at the centromere
	 - centrosome duplicated (animal cells only)
- G<sub>2</sub> :  The second gap
	 - final growth & preparation
	 - checks conditions & ==prepares for division==
	 - 2 centrosomes
- G<sub>0</sub> :  Resting phase
	 - ==dormancy==
	 - only for cells that don't divide/only divide under specific conditions
**M Phase**
- Mitosis - division of the ==nucleus==
	- *Prophase*
		 - ==chromatids condense== into visible chromosomes
		 - sister chromatids become distinct
		 - ==centrosomes move to opposite sides==
		 - miotic spindle begins to form
	- *Prometaphase*
		 - ==nuclear envelope breaks down==
		 - spindle fibers attach to chromosomes at the ==kinetochore==
	- *Metaphase*
		 - ==chromosomes line up along the metaphase plate== (middle)
		 - sister chromatids are positioned at the equatorial region of the cell
		 - spindle microtubules attach to the sister chromatids
	- *Anaphase*
		 - ==sister chromatids seperate== and are pulled towards opposite poles of the cell
	- *Telophase*
		 - nuclear envelopes form
		 - ==2 nuclei develop==
		 - chromosomes begin to condense
- Cytokinesis - division of the ==cytoplasm==

| phase         | time taken |
| ------------- | ---------- |
| G<sub>1</sub> | 4-5 hrs    |
| S             | 10-12 hrs  |
| G<sub>2</sub> | 4-6 hrs    |
| M             | ~1 hour    |
| *Total*       | ~24 hours  |

# KARYOTYPING
**Levels of DNA packaging**
`DNA -> nucleosome -> 30-nm chromatin fiber -> 300-nm chromatin fiber -> chromatid -> metaphase chromosome`
~2 meters of DNA fits into a nucleus under 10 μm thanks to packaging with histone proteins.
![[Pasted image 20260916052616.png|419]]
1. *DNA* - Each chromosome consists of a single long molecule of DNA
2. *Nucleosome* - is the basic unit of chromatin structure. Consists of DNA and a core of chromosomal proteins called ==histones==, which are a family of small `+` charged proteins which are capable of interacting with the `-` charged DNA. The 5 major types of histone proteins are ==H1, H2A, H2B, H3, & H4==
3. *30-nm chromatin fiber* - When nucleosomes are packed together, they form a 30-nm diameter chromatin fiber, or ==solenoid==, through the help of ==H1 histones==. H1 is bound to DNA as it enters and exits the nucleosome, pulling adjacent ones together into a repeating array.
   The solenoids are arranged in loops, anchored to nonhistone proteins called ==scaffolds==. Special regions on the DNA called ==scaffold attachment regions (SARs)==, bind to the scaffolds, leading to additional supercoiling and higher levels of organization
4. *300-nm chromatin fiber* - During prophase (mitosis), chromosomes need to condense into wider structures and decrease in length generating the condensed chromosomes during metaphase. The 30-nm structures are further folded into a series of looped domains that is 300-nm in diameter
5. *Chromatid* - The 300-nm chromatin fiber coils further to form one of the longitudinal subunits of the metaphase chromosome which is the chromatid that is 700-nm in diameter. The DNA assumes this particular organization during cell division

**Karyotyping** = sorting + arranging + identifying chromosomes by number, size, shape, centromere position, and banding.
*Karyotype* is an ordered display or diagram of the full set of an organism’s chromosomes
arranged in homologous pairs (two chromosomes except sex chromosomes with identical features i.e. length and centromere placement) with respect to size, shape, number, and banding pattern
*Humans* have 46 chromosomes = 22 autosome pairs + 1 sex pair

**Meiosis I**
- *Prophase I*
  - Leptotene - chromosomes are ==visible==
  - Zygotene - homonolgous chromosomes ==pair== through synapsis
  - Pachytene - Homologous chromosomes ==cross over==. Tetrads are present
  - Diplotene - Homologous chromosomes ==seperate==. Chiasmata becomes visible
  - Diakinesis - Chromosomes ==condense==. Nuclear membrane breaks down
- *Metaphase I*
  Tetrads ==align== at the equatorial plate
  Their orientation is random
- *Anaphase I*
  Homologous chromosomes ==seperate==
- *Telophase I*
  Chromosomes ==reach opposite poles== and the ==cell divides==

# ABNORMALITIES IN CELL DIVISION
**Tumors**
1. *Benign* - not cancerous & stays at original site
2. *Malignant* - invades + ==metastesizes== (spreads to distant sites)
**Proto-oncogene vs. Tumor suppresor**
- *Proto-oncogene* - ==accelerator== (normal growth)
- *Tumor supressor* - ==brake==

**Abnormal Chromosome Number**
Failure to separate: Meiosis I error = homologues fail to separate · Meiosis II/mitosis error = sister chromatids fail to separate. Results in ==monosomy (2n−1)== or ==trisomy (2n+1)==
   - *Aneuploidy of Autosomes*
     Trisomy **n** - means that there is an extra copy of chromosome **n**
     - Down Syndrome - (Trisomy 21; 47,XX/XY,+21)
       ==Upward slanting eyes==, open mouth with tongue protrusion, congenital heart defects, increased susceptibility to many diseases
     - Edwards Syndrome - (Trisomy 18; 47,XX/XY,+18)
       ==95% mortality rate==
     - Patau Syndrome - (Trisomy 13; 47,XX/XY,+13)
       ==Cleft lip== and cleft palate, polydactyl, low-set ears
   - *Aneuploidy of Sex Chromosomes*
     - Turner Syndrome - (Monosomy X; 45,XO)
       Short stature, webbed neck, immature sex organs, =="shield"-type chest==
     - Klinefelter Syndrome - (47,XXY)
       Severity of somatic malformations is ==proportional== to the ==number of additional X chromosomes==
     - Jacobs Syndrome - (47,XYY)
       Very tall and thin, Severe acne during adolescence, ==antisocial/behavioral problems==, learning disabilities, IQ is 10 to 15 points lower than siblings
     - Metafemale - (Trisomy X; 47,XXX)
       Fetal death rate is not notably higher than regular births, ==wide spaced eyes==, wide spaced nipples, abnormally-sized head (small/wide), often have learning disabilities
**Abnormal Chromosome Structure**
   - *Deletion*
     - Cri-du-chat Syndrome - French for "==cry of the cat=="
       Is due to a deletion in the short arm (p arm) of chromosome 5
       ==Cat-like/high pitched cry== caused by a malformation in the larynx, severe mental retardation, small heads, wide-set eyes, slow growth
   - *Translocation*
     - The Philadelphia Chromosome - happens when chromosome 9 and a large portion of the long arm (q arm) of chromosome 22 swap
       Associated with ==chronic myelogenous leukemia (CML)==
